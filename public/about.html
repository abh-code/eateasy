About EatEasy

A food and lifestyle discovery tool — INST 377, Spring 2026

What is EatEasy?

EatEasy helps people break out of their everyday cooking routines by discovering new recipes, finding local breweries, and pairing cocktails with their meals — all in one place. Built as a dynamic web application for INST 377 using three public APIs.

Built by

Amar Hassan · INST 377 · Spring 2026

How to Use EatEasy

1
Discover a Meal

Go to Meal Discovery. Click "Random" for inspiration, or search by ingredient.

2
Find a Brewery

Enter any U.S. city to get a list of local craft breweries.

3
Pair a Cocktail

Search by ingredient on the Cocktail Pairing page to browse and view full recipes.

APIs Used

🍲
TheMealDB

Recipes and cooking instructions from around the world

themealdb.com/api.php
🍺
Open Brewery DB

U.S. craft brewery data — search by city or state

openbrewerydb.org
🍹
TheCocktailDB

Cocktail recipes — search by ingredient or name

thecocktaildb.com/api.php
